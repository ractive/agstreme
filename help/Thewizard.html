The wizard

To show the "Wizard" dialog, click on the button in the toolbar

 

The operations

  • Update the ID3 tags of choosen files :
    With this option you can add tags to your MP3 files.
  • Strip ID3 tags from files:
    Remove all ID3 tags from choosen files. This is useful if they contain crap...
  • Search for a complete album on www.audiogalaxy.com:
    This will try to find all songs of a specific album on audiogalaxy. Unfortunately there's a bug in the CDDB control (not, it's not my fault... Write your complains to CDDB :-) that makes it hard to find mathing album informations :-(
    As soon as CDDB updates their control, I will integrate it into AGStreme...
  • Update the music database:
    This will build up the music database of all your songs.
