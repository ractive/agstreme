AGStreme 2.0

Welcome to JAGS

 

Napster's dead! Long live www.audiogalaxy.com!

Audiogalaxy is a sharing-platform for MP3 files with a HUGE community. You find every kind of music.

The idea how to handle file up- and downloads is in contrast to other sharing-platforms (like Napster (R.I.P.), kazaa, iMesh etc.) a bit different. The whole service is web-based. You search your songs on audiogalaxy's homepage and add them to your download queue within the webform. The client on your PC will then send and receive requested files.

The main reason why I started with JAGS is the fact, that the original client is not really "state of the art" software: too many bugs and too less comfort!

I hope JAGS will improve the whole audiogalaxy feeling :-)

Features

  • Built-in webbrowser for easy access to the most important audiogalaxy sites
  • Search result extractor. Songs from a search page are automatically extracted and filled in a search-list, where you can add them easily to your queue.
  • CDDB access (http://www.cddb.org )
  • Search for whole albums on CDDB
  • Full-featured, drag and drop enabled file-manager that lets you easily manage your MP3 files
  • ID3 tag (v1 and v2) support let's you view and modify song-information
  • Mass song tagging ! Scan your MP3 collection and update all ID3 tags with a few clicks
  • Music Database that shows your MP3 collection hierarchically, sorted by Artist/Album, Genre, Year
  • The mighty MP3-Wizard helps you with many tasks.
    • Clear all ID3 tags
    • Mass tag all your songs within minutes
    • Find related album information on CDDB and search for whole albums on audiogalaxy
    • Generate your music database
