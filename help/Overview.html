JAGS 0.9

Welcome to JAGS

 

Napster's dead! Long live www.audiogalaxy.com!

Audiogalaxy is a sharing-platform for MP3 files with a HUGE community. You find every kind of music.

The idea how to handle file up- and downloads is in contrast to other sharing-platforms (like Napster (R.I.P.), kazaa, iMesh etc.) a bit different. The whole service is web-based. You search your songs on audiogalaxy's homepage and add them to your download queue within the webform. The client on your PC will then send and receive requested files.

The main reason why I started with JAGS is the fact, that the original client is not really "state of the art" software: too many bugs and too less comfort!

I hope JAGS will improve the whole audiogalaxy feeling :-)
